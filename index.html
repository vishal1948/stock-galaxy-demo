      Returns & exchanges

      Items that come back get a credit note (CN) for what the customer paid - no cash back. It pays an exchange bill at once, or any later bill (payment mode "Credit note"). The stock comes back.

      0 = no limit. After the limit only the owner can take items back.

      Categories

      Up to 3 levels, e.g. Pants › Cargo pants › ₹500–999. Give each item its category in Stock In or Products. HSN and GST set on a category fill in for new items under it (a sub category without its own uses its parent's). A category used by items is switched off, not deleted, so old bills and reports keep its name.

      Data & safetywhere everything is saved, and the copies that keep it safe

      Data folder

      Everything is saved in this one folder - the database, bills, backups and Excel files - each in its own subfolder. Copy this folder to a pen drive now and then for safety.

      Main folder

      Backup & Google Drive copy

      Backups on this PC

      A complete copy of all data every hour, one file per day, last 30 days, in the Backups folder.

      Google Drive copy

      Keeps a copy of the backups, bills and Excel files in a Google Drive folder, so they are safe even if this PC is lost or its disk fails. Needs Google Drive for Desktop (free) installed and signed in. The live database itself is never put there - only finished copies.

      Restore

      Puts a backup back - from this PC or from the Google Drive copy, for example on a new PC. The data in use now is saved first, so a restore can be undone.

      Activation & support

      One shop key works on one computer. After activating, the app needs the internet at least once every 7 days. It tells your vendor its version, backup status, database size, last bill number and error count so problems can be fixed early. It never sends customer names, bills or sales.
